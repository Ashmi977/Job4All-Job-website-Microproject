SDG:10 - REDUCED INEQUALITY


SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities Established by the United Nations in 2015, SDG 10 is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals designed to create a more equitable and inclusive world.
Its mission is to:

  • Reduce income inequality
  • Promote social, economic, and political inclusion
  • Ensure equal opportunities and eliminate discriminatory practices


    • 👉🏼SDG:10 makes sure the following targets are accomplished:

    • Target 10.1: Promote faster income growth for the bottom 40% of the population.

    • Target 10.2: Ensure inclusive participation across all social, economic, and political spheres, regardless of identity or background.

    • Target 10.3: Eliminate discrimination and promote equal opportunities.

    • Target 10.6: Strengthen the representation of developing countries in global economic and financial decision-making. 



    🌐 Why SDG 10 Matters???
    
Reducing inequality is essential for:
- Promoting social cohesion and stability
- Enhancing economic growth
- Ensuring that no one is left behind in development efforts
