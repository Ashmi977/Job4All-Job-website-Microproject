How Job4All Works

Job4All connects inclusive employers with job seekers who need accessible workplaces. The process is simple for both sides — job seekers and employers.

For Job Seekers

  1. Search for jobs by role, type, or accessibility needs.
  2. Review accommodations such as remote work, ramps, flexible hours, or assistive tools.
  3. Apply directly with your resume and a short message.

For Employers

  1. Post a job with a clear title, description, and required skills.
  2. Add accessibility details and accommodations offered.
  3. Receive applications and connect with suitable candidates.
