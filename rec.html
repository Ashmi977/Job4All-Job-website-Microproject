Recommendations for Your Job Hunt

🎯 Customize Your Profile

Clearly mention your accessibility needs and preferred accommodations in your application or profile.

🔍 Use Smart Filters

Search jobs by location, role, and accommodations like remote work or wheelchair access.

📝 Highlight Your Strengths

Focus on your skills, experience, and adaptability. Use inclusive language and confident tone.

💬 Prepare for Accessible Interviews

Request interview accommodations in advance and practice responses that showcase your value.

📈 Track Your Applications

Keep a record of jobs applied to, responses received, and follow-up dates to stay organized.

🤝 Connect with Inclusive Employers

Look for companies that mention accessibility and diversity in their job descriptions and culture.
