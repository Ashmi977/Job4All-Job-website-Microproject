Why Job4All?

Job4All exists to ensure that everyone — regardless of disabilities — has equal access to meaningful employment, supportive workplaces, and fair professional opportunities.

Inclusive Hiring

We empower companies to create accessible roles and remove hiring barriers.

Visibility for Diverse Talent

Job seekers get a platform that recognizes and supports their skills and needs.

Accessible Workplaces

Listings include accommodations, ensuring job seekers pick roles that truly fit them.

Our Mission

To reduce inequality (aligned with SDG Goal 10) by promoting equal access to employment, encouraging accommodations, and fostering representation for people with disabilities.

Who We Serve

  • Job seekers with physical impairments
  • Deaf & hard-of-hearing applicants
  • Neurodivergent candidates
  • Individuals with learning disabilities
  • Anyone requiring workplace accommodations
